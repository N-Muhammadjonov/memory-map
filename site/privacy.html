Privacy

The downloaded app stores notes and attachments in an encrypted local vault. The download site does not receive the vault.

If enabled and accepted, the analytics service receives installation identifiers, version, platform, feature usage counts and approximate country. It does not receive note titles, content or passwords. Feedback and attached screenshots are sent only when you submit them.

Online account registration, when available, is handled by the account provider. The provider receives account email and credentials or Google sign-in information. The account does not upload your vault.

Update checks contact GitHub and optionally download verified release assets. A publicly shared note is sent only on your explicit publishing action.

For technical issues use GitHub Issues.
