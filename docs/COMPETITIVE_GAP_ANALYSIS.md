# Raqobatchilar va sinov uchun farqlar

Rasmiy manbalar 2026-11-21 kuni ko‘rib chiqildi. Narx bo‘yicha tarif tuzilishi berilgan. Hudud, hisob va to‘lov davri haqiqiy narxga ta’sir qiladi.

| Dastur | Tasdiqlangan ish oqimi va cheklovlari | Xotira uchun taqqoslash vazifasi | Manbalar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notion | Ma’lumotlar bazasi, shakllar, avtomatlashtirish, jamoa ruxsatlari va AI tariflari. Yuklangan sahifalar mobil va desktopda oflayn ishlaydi | Loyihani import qilish, muddatni ko‘rinishlar orasida o‘zgartirish, internetsiz kirish | [Oflayn](https://www.notion.com/help/use-pages-offline), [avtomatlashtirish](https://www.notion.com/help/database-automations), [shakllar](https://www.notion.com/help/forms), [narx](https://www.notion.com/pricing) |
| Obsidian | Mahalliy Markdown, JSON Canvas, plugin SDK, pulli Sync va Publish xizmatlari | Havola, alias va faylni saqlagan qayta eksport. Sinxronlashda tiklanish | [Saqlash](https://obsidian.md/help/data-storage), [Canvas](https://obsidian.md/help/Plugins/Canvas), [SDK](https://docs.obsidian.md/), [narx](https://obsidian.md/pricing.html) |
| Heptabase | Vizual ish maydoni, import, tarix, mobil ilova va AI tariflari. | Capacities | Obyekt turlari, eksport, sinxronlanadigan oflayn ish, pulli AI. | Anytype | Mahalliy-first, qurilmadan chiqishdan oldingi shifrlash va tarifga bog‘liq hamkorlik yoki saqlash | Qulf, ko‘chirish, ikki qurilmada tahrirlash va tiklanish | [FAQ](https://anytype.io/faq/), [texnika](https://tech.anytype.io/), [narx](https://anytype.io/pricing/) |
| Tana Outliner | Supertag maydonlari, mobil capture, tarifga bog‘liq ovozli va AI vositalari | Telefon orqali fikr yozish va uni loyiha bilan bog‘lash | [Mobil](https://outliner.tana.inc/learn/features/tana-mobile), [tariflar](https://outliner.tana.inc/pricing) |
| Logseq | Ochiq kod, outliner va graf. Rasmiy repoda DB yo‘nalishi beta, yangi mobil yoki RTC yo‘nalishi alfa deb ko‘rsatilgan | Oflayn kunlik qayd, tez qidiruv, qayta importda havolalar | [Repo](https://github.com/logseq/logseq), [DB hujjati](https://github.com/logseq/docs/blob/master/db-version.md) |
| Roam Research | Bog‘langan fikrlash uchun graflar va rejalar. Rasmiy sahifada mobil bo‘lim ham bor | Ikki yo‘nalishli bog‘lanish va fikrdan manbaga qaytish | [Mahsulot va rejalar](https://roamresearch.com/) |

Hujjatda oflayn rejim yo‘qligi qayd etilgan | Katta taxtada orientatsiya va internet uzilgandagi harakat | [Talablar](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017731553-System-requirements), [rejalar](https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017730233-Plans-and-features-available) |

Obsidian sharhlarida sozlash va mobil sinxronlashning o‘rganish xarajati aytiladi. Notion Oflayn sharhi muallifning tajribasini ta’riflaydi. Bular reprezentativ so‘rov yoki platformalararo xavfsizlik auditi hisoblanmaydi. [G2 sharhlari](https://www.g2.com/products/obsidian/reviews), [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/notion-offline-mode-vs-obsidian-3590238/).

Mahsulot gipotezasi. Boshlang‘ich omborni tezroq qayta import qilish va aloqani tezroq tushunish talaba, tadqiqotchi va mustaqil ishlab chiquvchi uchun o‘lchanadigan ustunlik berishi mumkin. Mezonga kiradigan vazifalar, tanlov va xolis baholash [UX qabul mezonlarida](UX_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md).
